2026 Gulf Crisis: Macroeconomic Impact AnalysisOverviewThis project presents a rigorous panel data econometric analysis investigating the macroeconomic transmission mechanisms of the 2026 Gulf War oil shock. The study examines how oil price volatility, exchange rates, and geopolitical uncertainty impacted inflation (CPI) across a panel of four diverse economies: the USA, Italy, Saudi Arabia, and Turkey. 
Key Technical Highlights
Methodology: Panel Data Analysis utilizing Fixed Effects (FE) estimators, validated against Pooled OLS and Random Effects. 
Robustness: Implementation of Arellano (1987) clustered standard errors to correct for heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation. 
Diagnostic Rigor: Sequential diagnostic testing using Breusch-Pagan and Hausman tests to justify model selection. 
Key Findings: The analysis reveals that the Nominal Effective Exchange Rate (NEER) serves as the primary transmission channel for inflationary pressure, rather than the direct price of oil.
